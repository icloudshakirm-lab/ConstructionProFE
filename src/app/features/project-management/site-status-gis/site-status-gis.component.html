How to use

  1. Preloaded data — roads-seoul.geojson (OSM roads). Segments are grouped into progress layers (survey, excavation, backfill, sub-base, compaction, pipe work, asphalt, etc.).
  2. Layers panel — on the right, check or uncheck each stage to show or hide those road segments on the map.
  3. Draw more — pick a progress type, then Line (from–to) to add reaches; export includes progressStatus and roadName.

Data source: OpenStreetMap via Overpass (Seoul road sample). Layer assignment is illustrative for demo — adjust on site or replace with your survey / as-built GeoJSON.
